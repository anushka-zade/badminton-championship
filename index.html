HOW LEAGUE POINTS WORK

Win = 2 points. Loss = 0 points. These points rank teams in the league table; they are separate from the score played in each match.

Tie-breakers: head-to-head record, points difference, then points scored. If a tie still affects qualification, the organiser will arrange a golden-point decider.

Road to the championship

Semifinal teams and finalists are determined automatically.

Semi-final 1: Rank 1 vs Rank 4. Semi-final 2: Rank 2 vs Rank 3. The two semifinal winners meet in the Grand Final.

Championship rules

Simple, fair and competitive.

  1. 01League stage: All six teams play each of the other five teams once. There are 15 league matches.
  2. 02Match format: Every match is one game to 15 points.
  3. 03Scoring: Rally scoring will be followed.
  4. 04Golden point: At 14–14, the next point wins the match. There will be no deuce or advantage.
  5. 05League points: 2 points for a win and 0 points for a loss. No draws.
  6. 06Semifinals: The top four teams qualify. Rank 1 plays Rank 4, and Rank 2 plays Rank 3.
  7. 07Grand Final: The semifinal winners compete for the championship. The final winner is the tournament champion.
  8. 08Leaderboard: Teams are ranked by league points, head-to-head record, points difference and points scored. A golden-point decider resolves any remaining tie affecting qualification.
  9. 09Team names: To change your team name, please contact admin Anushka. Changes require admin approval.
  10. 10Fair play: Standard badminton doubles rules apply. The organiser makes the final call on disputes.
  11. 11Prizes: Winner and runner-up prizes will be announced separately.
